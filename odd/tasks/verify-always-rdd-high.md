# Verify always runs by risk; RDD runs only for high-risk candidates

Verification follows the risk tier whether RDD is on or off; RDD becomes the external, isolated
four-lens view that runs automatically only when the native assessment says `high`. Applies to
gentle-ai (shared guidance, contract, hooks) and Gentle Shell (gentle-pi assets, assess plan).
